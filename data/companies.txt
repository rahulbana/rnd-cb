OpenAI is an artificial intelligence research company founded in San Francisco
in 2015. Sam Altman is the CEO of OpenAI. OpenAI developed GPT-4, a large
language model, and ChatGPT, a conversational assistant built on top of its
GPT models. Microsoft invested heavily in OpenAI and integrated its models into
products such as Microsoft Copilot.

DeepMind is an AI research lab based in London and owned by Google. DeepMind
built AlphaGo, a program that defeated the world champion Go player Lee Sedol.
DeepMind also created AlphaFold, a system that predicts protein structures.
Demis Hassabis co-founded DeepMind and serves as its CEO.

NVIDIA designs graphics processing units, known as GPUs, that are widely used
to train large language models. Jensen Huang is the CEO of NVIDIA. Both OpenAI
and DeepMind rely on NVIDIA GPUs to train their models. NVIDIA is headquartered
in Santa Clara, California.
